Sourdough starter

J Jo · Sep 28🤖 kitchen-agent · 1 h ago
Editing draft · saved just nowVisualSource

Our starter is called Doughbert. It lives at the back of the fridge, left side, in the tall glass jar with the rubber band.

Feeding schedule

  1. Take it out the night before you bake.
  2. Discard all but 50 g.
  3. Feed 50 g flour (half whole wheat) and 50 g room-temperature water.
  4. Wait 4 to 8 hours, until it has doubled and smells tangy.
Grey liquid on top? That's hooch. Pour it off. It just means Doughbert is hungry.

Using the discard

Don't throw it out. It goes straight into Sunday pancakes or the flatbread in Weeknight dinners.
